Sun Mountain builds durable, high-performance golf bags, travel gear, and apparel engineered to perform in real conditions and stand the test of time.

Our Purpose

We exist to earn trust through gear that performs when conditions demand it, helping golfers play their best with confidence they can rely on round after round.

Our Vision

To be the most respected performance brand in golf, built on the uncompromising TQR Standard and rooted in authentic golf.

Our Mission

To set the standard for trust, quality, and reliability in golf equipment.

THE TQR STANDARD

Our brand is built on three pillars

Trust

Trust is earned through performance that holds up over time, delivering gear golfers can rely on in the conditions they actually play, when it matters most.

Quality

Quality comes from purpose-built design, durable materials, and craftsmanship engineered for the realities of golf, ensuring every product performs as intended on the course and off.

Reliability

Reliability is built into every product, ensuring consistent performance through the demands of the game, round after round.

VOICE & TONE

We speak like the gear we build.

Our Voice

Built Like Gear

We describe how things are built, how they work, and how they hold up.

  • Equipment, not fashion
  • Focus on construction, materials, and durability
  • Designed for real conditions

Trusted Performance

We communicate performance through proof, not claims.

  • Confidence comes from consistency over time
  • Show how it performs, not just what it is
  • Reliability is implied, not exaggerated

Golf-First Movement

Everything connects back to the game.

  • The swing
  • The walk
  • The round
  • The conditions

Protection, performance, and movement should always tie back to how golf is actually played.

Our Tone

Do

  • Be direct and plainspoken
  • Use short, clear sentences
  • Be specific (what it does, how it works)
  • Use strong verbs
  • Show quiet confidence

Don’t

  • Use flowery or poetic language
  • Over-polish or over-style the writing
  • Use superlatives without proof (“best-in-class”)
  • Use generic claims (“advanced technology”)
  • Lean into lifestyle-first language (“harmony,” “effortless”)

Core Vocabulary

TQR Language

Proven, trusted, dependable, steady, consistent, built to count on, time-tested.

Rugged Quality

Hardwearing, purpose-built, built-right, engineered, made to last.

Weather Authority

Storm-ready, weather-ready, sealed, locked-in, sheds water, stays dry.

System Language

Built as a system, no weak links, gap-free coverage, overlaps where it counts.

Golf Movement

Moves naturally, unrestricted, balanced.
