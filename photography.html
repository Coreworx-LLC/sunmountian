Photography Overview

Sun Mountain photography reflects real golf.

We show the game as it’s actually played—early mornings, changing weather, long walks, and the moments where performance matters most.

Our imagery is grounded, natural, and purposeful. Nothing is staged to feel perfect. Everything is captured to feel true.

We prioritize movement over posing, real conditions over controlled environments, and performance over aesthetics.

Products should be shown in use—carried, worn, tested, and relied on.

Every image should reinforce trust, quality, and reliability.

Principles

  • Authentic golf, always
  • Movement over posing
  • Real conditions over ideal conditions
  • Show performance and durability

Lifestyle

Lifestyle imagery shows golf as it’s actually experienced.

Early tee times, changing weather, long walks, and the rhythm of the round. Nothing staged to feel perfect. Everything captured to feel true.

We prioritize real golfers, natural movement, and environments that reflect how the game is played.

Products should always be part of the moment—carried, worn, and used without disruption. They support the round, not distract from it.

Principles

  • Real golfers, not posed models
  • Movement over stillness
  • Natural environments over staged settings
  • Capture the walk, not just the swing
  • Show gear in use, not just in frame

Talent

Authentic golfers who are happy, healthy, and relatable to our audience. We don’t want models; we want real characters and genuine expressions.

We also represent all golfers, so a realistic mix of age, gender and ethnicity is key. But it’s not about hitting a quota, choosing the best talent comes first.

Product

E-commerce product photography is to be taken in a studio setting, with soft shadows and placed on the referenced grey color background.
